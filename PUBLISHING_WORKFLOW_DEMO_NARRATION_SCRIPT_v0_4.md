# Publishing Workflow Demo Narration v0.4

Render the seven sections with `node render_demo_audio.mjs`. The approved profile is Kokoro `am_fenrir` at speed `0.83`, with controlled silence between sections. Human listening and approval remain required before publication.

## 1 — The burden

Publishing one idea across scripts, captions, video, and platform copy can exhaust disabled and energy-limited creators.

## 2 — Recovery-Aware Mode

Recovery-Aware Mode starts with a user-selected minute budget. It automates mechanical work, schedules what fits, and saves deferred decisions in a resume checkpoint.

## 3 — Source custody

A manifest locks approved source, audio, timing, brand assets, and review status. A second logo-free case proves the runner is reusable.

## 4 — Enforced boundaries

The runner blocks changed inputs, unapproved narration, unsafe captions, timing errors, path escapes, and automated approval. It also hashes every finished output.

## 5 — Working result

The first build is a thirty-six point nine second vertical Short with source-bound narration, ten caption cues, a media probe, and visual Q A.

## 6 — Codex contribution

Codex with G P T five point six Sol generalized the runner, built Recovery Mode, and expanded the suite to fifteen passing tests across two cases.

## 7 — Human authority

Artificial intelligence reduces repetitive production and physical strain. The creator keeps meaning, rights, approval, and publication authority.

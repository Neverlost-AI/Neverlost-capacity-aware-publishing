## Title

Two Patients. Opposite Realities. The Same Paradox. | Neverlost

## Description

Some patients have social support but struggle for medical recognition. Others gain medical recognition while losing the stability and support needed to act on it.

Both realities can be true. Both patients may be searching for the same thing: stability.

This Short is adapted from *The Patient's Paradox*, a chapter from *The Modern Paradox* and part of the philosophical foundation for Neverlost.

Watch the full chapter: https://youtu.be/eST8JpADyYY

Narration uses an AI-assisted voice. The manuscript, framework, lived-experience perspective, and editorial direction are Neverlost's.

This work presents lived experience and systems thinking. It is not medical, legal, benefits, or vocational rehabilitation advice.
